Compensation Cards Design System

Example 1: Medical & Treatment Compensation

Emergency Care & Hospitalization

Emergency room visits, hospital stays, specialist consultations, and follow-up medical treatment

Future Medical Needs

Surgery, rehabilitation, specialist care, and ongoing medical monitoring

Rehabilitation & Therapy

Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and long-term pain management

Lost Wages

Lost income and reduced future earning capacity due to injury

Pain & Suffering

Emotional distress, anxiety, PTSD, and loss of enjoyment of life

Property Damage

Damage to your vehicle and personal property

Example 2: Spinal Injury Compensation

Emergency Care & Treatment

Emergency care, hospitalisation, and specialist treatment for spinal injuries

Imaging & Diagnostics

MRI, CT scans, X-rays, and ongoing medical monitoring

Rehabilitation & Pain Management

Physical therapy and long-term pain management programs

Mobility Devices

Future surgeries, mobility devices, and assistive technology

Loss of Earning Capacity

Lost earnings and reduced future earning capacity

Home Modifications

Home modifications and in-home care support

Emotional Distress

Emotional distress, trauma, and loss of enjoyment of life

Example 3: Wrongful Death Compensation

Medical Treatment Costs

Medical treatment and care costs prior to death

Loss of Income

Loss of income and financial support for surviving family members

Emotional Suffering

Emotional suffering, grief, and mental anguish

Funeral Expenses

Funeral and burial expenses

Loss of Companionship

Loss of companionship and support for family members

Property Damage

Other injuries or property damage resulting from the accident

Example 4: Truck Accident Case Evidence

Medical Treatment Records

Medical treatment received immediately after the accident

Police Reports & Footage

Police reports, eyewitnesses and dashcam footage

Lost Wages Documentation

Lost wages or time off work documentation

Rehabilitation Records

Rehabilitation and therapy you may be undergoing

Pain & Suffering

Documentation of pain and suffering experienced

Emotional Distress

Any emotional distress, PTSD, or loss of enjoyment of life

Vehicle & Property Damage

Damage to your vehicle and personal property

Visible Injuries

Disfigurement or visible scarring as a result of the accident

Insurance Contact

Contact having been made by the at-fault party's insurer
