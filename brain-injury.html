What Counts as a Traumatic Brain Injury?

Not every head injury qualifies as a traumatic brain injury under California law. A valid and strong brain injury lawsuit typically involves injuries that significantly alter a person's cognitive, physical, or emotional functioning.

At Peerali Law, traumatic brain injury cases commonly involve:

  • Loss of consciousness or prolonged confusion
  • Memory loss, cognitive impairment, or personality changes
  • Diagnosed concussions with ongoing symptoms
  • Brain bleeding, swelling, or oxygen deprivation
  • Permanent neurological deficits or developmental delays
  • Injuries requiring long-term treatment or lifelong care

These cases require more than proving negligence. They demand a detailed explanation of how the injury affects every aspect of the victim's future. This level of case development is where our firm focuses its expertise.
