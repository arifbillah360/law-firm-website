How Do I Know I Have a Trucking Accident Case?

Not every injury sustained in a truck accident can lead a successful claim. However, certain factors validate and strengthen a lawsuit. At Peerali Law, when consulting with clients following a truck accident injury we will ask for details surrounding:

  • Medical treatment received immediately after the accident
  • Police reports, eyewitnesses and dashcam footage
  • Lost wages or time off work
  • Rehabilitation and therapy you may be undergoing
  • Pain and suffering
  • Any emotional distress, PTSD, or loss of enjoyment of life
  • Damage to your vehicle and personal property
  • Disfigurement or visible scarring as a result of the accident
  • Contact having been made by the at-fault party's insurer

In order to prepare a strong case, the attorney must not only prove liability but also quantify the entire future impact of the injury.

If you're not sure about your situation, we can review your case for free and tell you exactly where you stand.
