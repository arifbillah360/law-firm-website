About Serena

Serena Peerali is the Co-Founding Partner of Peerali Law and a dedicated advocate for individuals and families facing the devastating impact of catastrophic injuries. Known for her compassionate approach and unwavering dedication, Serena has earned a reputation as an attorney who truly cares about her clients' well-being and recovery.

Since co-founding Peerali Law in 2021, Serena has been instrumental in building the firm's client-centered culture. She works directly with clients throughout their cases, ensuring they receive personalized attention and clear communication at every stage of the legal process.

Serena's practice focuses on catastrophic injury cases, wrongful death claims, and complex personal injury litigation. She has particular expertise in cases involving long-term care needs, permanent disability, and claims requiring extensive medical documentation and expert testimony.
